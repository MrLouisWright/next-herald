# next-herald wiki

**Typed metadata, XSS-safe JSON-LD, sitemaps with hreflang, OG templates and an SEO CI check for the Next.js 16 App Router, legible to AI search with no ranking or AI-citation claims. Zero dependencies beyond `next` and `react`.**

> [!WARNING]
> **Pre-alpha, not published to npm.** All APIs are planned. The canonical source is [`docs/`](https://github.com/MrLouisWright/next-herald/blob/main/docs/README.md); this wiki mirrors it.
